# Roberto Malvessi

**The Dread Roberto Malvessi** — Commodore of the [[Malvessi]] Armada and the most feared corsair name on the Rock. A legendary, dreaded pirate whose raids are still sung (and cursed) from Greyspace to Realmspace. Merchants whisper that half the shipwrecks in the Flow trace back to his tricorne. On Bral, captains answer to his name alone — or they answer to the airlock.
