# Vittoria Serpes
Vittoria is a high-society gorgon "sculptor" with very above-board ties to the [[Zenonni]] and collections sponsored by [[Il Velluto]]. 

Her work is more around posing and composition rather than sculting rock, as the actual pieces are petrified people. But Vittoria is not a serial petrifier; in fact, her work is so well-regarded people *volunteer* to be pieces and get paid a share of the absurdly pricy (and temporary) exhibit. 

Yes: people will get paid to be petrified in a rich person's house for a couple of weeks and the patron will happily liberate the piece after the novelty fades away, filling the subject's and Miss Serpes' pockets in return.
