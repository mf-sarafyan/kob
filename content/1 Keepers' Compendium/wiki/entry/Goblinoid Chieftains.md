# Goblinoid Chieftains

Before the [[Unhuman Wars|Inhuman Wars]], [[Gobbspace]] was divided among numerous goblin, hobgoblin and bugbear peoples ruled by competing chieftains, warlords and petty kings.

The strongest states were generally dominated by highly militarized hobgoblin elites, while enormous populations of goblins provided the miners, craftsmen, farmers, sailors and laborers that kept their realms functioning.

During the [[Unhuman Wars|Inhuman Wars]], these powers fought the elves and lost.

The peace imposed afterward prohibited the goblinoids of the sphere from developing or possessing spelljamming technology.

It did not, however, prohibit them from going to space.
