# Scro

The Scro are the descendants of orcs who remained in the devastated [[Khanspace]] after the [[Unhuman Wars|Inhuman Wars]].

They rebuilt themselves around a deliberate rejection of everything they believed had doomed the old orcs: disunity, ignorance, impulsiveness and endless internal warfare. Scro society places enormous value on education, discipline, patience, courtesy and self-control.

As a result, Scro have a reputation as remarkably pleasant people. Their communities are orderly and hospitable, their soldiers professional, and open aggression is generally considered crude.

They have also never forgotten who destroyed the [[The Great Khanate|Great Khanate]].
