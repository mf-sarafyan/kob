# Grand Goblin Assembly

The prohibition on spelljamming transformed [[Gobbspace]].

Unable to rely upon helms to travel between worlds, its peoples constructed increasingly enormous networks of mines, foundries, railways, factories and eventually mundane spacecraft. Industrialization placed tremendous power in the hands of the workers upon whom the entire system depended.

Eventually, those workers organized.

What began as labor unions grew into a sphere-wide political movement that displaced the old [[Goblinoid Chieftains|chieftains]] and established the Grand Goblin Assembly.

Modern Gobbspace is consequently one of the strangest civilizations in Wildspace: a heavily industrialized goblinoid workers' republic whose rockets, orbital stations and interplanetary infrastructure operate almost entirely without spelljamming magic.
