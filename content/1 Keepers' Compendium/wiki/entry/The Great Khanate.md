# The Great Khanate

Long before the [[Unhuman Wars|Inhuman Wars]], orcs united under a succession of great Khans and built one of the major powers of Wildspace. Descended from nomadic conquerors, the Khanate carried the traditions of the steppe into space: fleets became herds, ships became steeds, and the void became an endless grassland to cross.

The Great Khanate eventually came into conflict with the elves during the [[Unhuman Wars|Inhuman Wars]]. The [[Elven Imperial Navy]] devastated [[Khanspace]] so thoroughly that orc civilization there was effectively reduced to the Stone Age.

The Khanate never recovered.

Its descendants did.
