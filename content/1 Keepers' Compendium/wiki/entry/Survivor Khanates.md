# Survivor Khanates

Not every heir of the [[The Great Khanate|Great Khanate]] remained in [[Khanspace]].

Some surviving clans returned completely to the nomadic traditions of their ancestors. Their fleets wander between spheres aboard elaborate wooden ships modeled after horses, carrying entire communities with them. Each is ruled by its own Khan, and alliances between these wandering Khanates are temporary, personal and frequently complicated.

Many believe the Great Khanate's fatal mistake was settling down: cities, shipyards and capitals merely gave the elves something they could find and destroy.

Their answer is simple.

Never stop riding.
