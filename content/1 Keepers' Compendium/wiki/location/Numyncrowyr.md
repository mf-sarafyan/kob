# Numyncrowyr

Planar region of the [[Astral Sea]] bordering the Phlogiston at [[The Red Tide]].

That’s a brilliant concept! The **Crimson Torrent** as a river of blood from a dying, forgotten deity adds a layer of **mystery** and **cosmic tragedy** to the Astral Sea. Here are some ideas for how to flesh out this concept:

### **The Crimson Torrent: Blood of a Forgotten Deity**
The **Crimson Torrent** could be the **remnants of a divine entity**, a **god** or **cosmic being** who was slain or whose power faded into oblivion. The blood of this being flows through the Astral Sea, a reminder of a **cosmic struggle**, an **ancient betrayal**, or the **slow decay of a once-great deity**.

#### 1. **The Nature of the Torrent**
- **Divine Essence**: The **blood** that flows through the Crimson Torrent is not just physical but also **divine essence**. It’s imbued with **raw cosmic power**, which may be harnessed by those who know how to manipulate it. This blood could have **magical properties**, possibly even granting power to those who drink it (though with dire consequences).
  
- **A Fragmented Deity**: The **forgotten deity** whose blood now fills the torrent could have been **dismembered** or **killed** during a cataclysmic event. Their **consciousness** could still linger in the flow, a fractured and maddened version of the god that haunts those who venture too deep into the river. This entity could be **calling out** to those who pass by, offering dark **blessings** or tempting them with **power**.

#### 2. **The Impact on the Multiverse**
- **Magical Distortions**: The blood of this forgotten god could distort the **flow of magic** in the region. It might cause **wild magic surges**, **mutations**, or the emergence of **corrupted creatures**. These magical distortions could be the result of the deity’s waning influence on the multiverse, leaking through the currents of the **Crimson Torrent**.

- **Cosmic Echoes**: The **death of the deity** might have created a **cosmic rift** that reverberates across the planes. The **Crimson Torrent** could be a **conduit** for these **echoes**, and those who sail it might experience strange visions, memories of the god’s past, or even confront **manifestations** of their forgotten divine power.

- **A Breeding Ground for Chaos**: Demons and Devils, sensing the power in the Torrent, might try to claim it as their own. They could see the **blood** as a **source of corruption** or a **weapon** to wield against their rivals. The **Keepers of the Balance** could be tasked with stopping one faction from harnessing the blood's power and disrupting the multiverse’s stability.

#### 3. **Adventuring Opportunities**
- **Divine Relics**: Scattered throughout the Crimson Torrent could be **fragments of divine relics** or **artifacts** tied to the forgotten deity. The Keepers might be sent on a quest to **recover these relics** before they fall into the wrong hands, or perhaps to **destroy them** to prevent the god’s blood from being exploited for nefarious purposes.

- **Exploring the Torrent**: The **Keepers of the Balance** could journey into the heart of the **Crimson Torrent** to find out more about the deity’s death and uncover secrets long buried. They might encounter **astral predators**, **temporal anomalies**, and **god-touched creatures** that have been corrupted by the blood.

- **A God’s Final Request**: What if the **forgotten deity**, in its last moments, left behind a **final message** or **quest**? The Keepers could discover this message through strange **prophecies** or cryptic **visions** while traveling the Torrent, perhaps urging them to **restore the deity** or prevent its memory from being lost forever.

- **Divine Intervention**: While traveling the Torrent, the Keepers might be able to **invoke** the god’s fragmented consciousness, gaining **brief glimpses** of lost knowledge or **temporary boons** of divine power. However, this might come at a cost: a **dark price** that may slowly twist the Keepers’ fate.

#### 4. **Rival Factions**
- **Devils and Demons**: Both **Devils** and **Demons** might covet the blood for its power, leading to **open warfare** or **clandestine operations** within the Torrent. Perhaps the **Keepers** have to act as **mediators** between the two sides or stop them from turning the entire **astral flow** into a **battleground** for dominance.

- **The Cult of the Forgotten Deity**: A **cult** might have formed around the remnants of this **forgotten god**, believing that by drinking the god’s blood or invoking its name, they can **revive** it or gain access to its divine power. The Keepers could have to confront this **cult** and prevent them from completing their twisted rituals.

- **Astral Pirates**: **Pirates** or **mercenaries** who roam the Crimson Torrent might seek to **plunder** its divine blood, using it as a currency or tool to gain power. Some might even be **siphoning the blood** into bottles or flasks to sell to the highest bidder, potentially causing **chaos** in the multiverse.

### **Environmental Features of the Crimson Torrent**
- **Bloody Storms**: The torrent might be plagued by **storms of blood**, swirling and crashing through the currents, causing intense **magical disturbances**. These storms could appear suddenly, sweeping ships off course or forcing travelers to seek shelter in safe zones.
  
- **Blood Tides**: The flow of the **Crimson Torrent** might not be constant but influenced by **astral tides** or the passing of certain celestial bodies. As the tides of blood rise and fall, the strength of the current could fluctuate, creating dangerous rapids or calm periods of eerie silence.

- **Divine Remains**: The depths of the Torrent could contain the **remains of the forgotten god**—fragments of **flesh**, **bone**, or **celestial organs** that have become entangled with the astral currents. These remains could be valuable (or dangerous) to scholars and treasure hunters.

- **A Living River**: The **Crimson Torrent** might not just be a river of blood but a **living, breathing entity** that feeds off the life essence of those who enter it. Some creatures, perhaps twisted and corrupted by the god’s blood, may inhabit the Torrent, offering further threats to explorers.

### **Tie-In with the Blood War**
The **Crimson Torrent** could be a powerful tool in the **Blood War**, a source of untapped divine energy that both **Demons** and **Devils** seek to control. Perhaps **Mordenkainen** sends the **Keepers** on a quest to prevent the blood from falling into the hands of one side or the other, or maybe he sends them to ensure the **secret history** of the god’s death stays hidden, so that its power isn’t revived to tip the scales in the Blood War.

This approach makes the **Crimson Torrent** a **powerful, mysterious force** in your campaign, not just a backdrop for battles but a central component of the **cosmic struggle** that the Keepers of the Balance must contend with.
