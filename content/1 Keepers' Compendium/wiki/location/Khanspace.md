# Khanspace

The ancestral sphere of the orcs and former heartland of the [[The Great Khanate|Great Khanate]]. The Khanate was destroyed during the [[Unhuman Wars|Inhuman Wars]], and Khanspace is now dominated by the [[Scro]], a peaceful, highly organized orc society that rebuilt among its ruins.

See also: [[Survivor Khanates]] (heirs who left the sphere), [[The Great Khanate]], [[Scro]].
