## Paladin
- **(1) Lay on Hands (25 HP pool).**
	- Action: heal or cure disease/poison.

- **(1) Weapon Mastery.**
	- Longsword — Vex
	- Warhammer — Push
	- Javelin — Slow

- **(1) Spellcasting.**
	- Spell Save DC: 14
	- Spell Attack: +6

- **(2) Fighting Style: Defense.**
	- +1 AC while wearing armor

- **(2) Divine Smite.**
	- On hit, expend spell slot to deal radiant damage

- **(3) Sacred Oath (Devotion).**
	- **Sacred Weapon**
	- **Turn the Unholy**

- **(4) Feat: Shield Master.**
	- Bonus Action: Shove a creature within 5 ft using your shield
	- Add shield AC bonus to Dex saves vs effects that target only you
	- On successful Dex save for half damage: take no damage instead

- **(5) Extra Attack.**

# Special: Tower Shield
- +2 AC
- Heavy, Special
- Requires STR 13+
- Speed -5 ft while wielding
- **Brace** (Bonus Action)
> Until the start of your next turn:
>- You gain **half cover** (+2 AC, +2 Dex saves)
>- Allies of your choice within 5 ft gain **half cover**
>- You cannot move more than 5 ft
- **Interpose** (Reaction)
>Trigger: A creature within 5 ft is hit
>- Grant +2 AC vs the triggering attack
>- If it still hits, you may become the target instead
- **Shield Wall** (Action)
>You can use this feature only if you haven’t moved during this turn
> After you use it, your Speed is 0 until the end of the current turn
>Until the start of your next turn:
>- You gain **three-quarters cover** (+5 AC, +5 Dex saves)
>- Allies of your choice within 5 ft gain **half cover**
