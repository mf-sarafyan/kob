# Traits
## Race / Feats
- **One-Eyed.**
	- Kurrzot struggles with depth perception.
	- Disadvantage on Perception checks.
	- Disadvantage on attack rolls against targets more than 10 ft away.

- **Large Frame.**
	- Kurrzot is a Large creature.
	- Occupies a 10x10 ft space.
	- Cannot wear normal humanoid armor unless specially crafted for Large creatures.
	- Gains a lot of health. 
	- Carrying, pushing, dragging, lifting capacity greatly increased.
	- Smaller creatures may enter his space and climb onto him.

- **Adorable.**
	- Advantage on Charisma checks when speaking his native Deep Speech.

- **Big Hands.**
	- Unarmed strikes deal `1d6 + STR`.
	- When Kurrzot hits a Large or smaller creature with an unarmed strike, he may automatically grapple it.
	- Grappled creatures have disadvantage on attacks against creatures other than Kurrzot.

## Fighter
- **(1) Fighting Style: Protection (mod).**
	- When a creature you can see attacks a target other than you within 5 ft of you, use Reaction to impose disadvantage on the attack roll.

- **(1) Second Wind (2 uses).**
	- Bonus Action: regain `1d10 + 5` HP.
	- Recover all on Long Rest; recover 1 on Short Rest.

- **(1) Weapon Mastery.**
	- 4 mastered weapons:
		- Warhammer — Push
		- Spear — Sap
		- Javelin — Slow
		- Longsword — Sap
		- Also unarmed attacks I guess. 

- **(2) Action Surge (1).**
	- Take one additional action on your turn.
	- Recharges on Short or Long Rest.

- **(2) Tactical Mind.**
	- On failed ability check, expend Second Wind use to add `1d10`.
	- If still fail, use is not spent.
	- O flavor é ele sendo teimoso?

- **(3) Battle Master Maneuvers.**
	- Maneuver Save DC = 15
	- Maneuver Dice = d8
	- 4 dice per Short or Long Rest

	Known Maneuvers:
	- **Bait and Switch.**
		Switch places with ally within 5 ft; one gains +1d8 AC until next turn.
	- **Maneuvering Attack.**
		On hit, add 1d8 damage; ally can move half speed without OA.
	- **Rally.**
		Bonus Action: ally gains temp HP = 1d8 + CHA mod + Fighter level.
	- **Goading Attack.**
		On hit, add 1d8 damage; target has disadvantage attacking others.

- **(4) ASI / Feat.**
	- +1 Strength, +1 Constitution
	- STR 19, CON 16 already included.

- **(5) Extra Attack.**
	- Attack twice when taking Attack action.

- **(5) Tactical Shift.**
	- When using Second Wind, move up to half speed without provoking OA.
