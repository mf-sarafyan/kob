# Traits

## Special Feat — Bhaal’s Champion
- **Aura of Murder (Modified).**
  - Ignore resistance to **piercing damage** against creatures within 5 ft. **Immune** creatures take half damage instead.
- **Shadow Step (Action).**
  - Become **invisible** until end of next turn or until you attack.
- **Haruspex.**
  - You can make a divining ritual over the course of one minute (can be during a short rest) if you have a corpse available, and see glimpses of the future in its entrails. Roll a d20 and note it down. Until your next long rest, before a d20 is rolled, you can choose to replace its result with the number you noted. 
  - Use once/long rest.

## Rogue (Assassin)
- **Expertise (2 skills).**
- **Sneak Attack (2d6).**
- **Thieves’ Cant.**
- **Weapon Mastery**. Rapier, Dagger.
- **Cunning Action.**
  - Dash, Disengage, Hide as Bonus Action.
- **Steady Aim.**
	- As a Bonus Action, you give yourself Advantage on your next attack roll on the current turn. You can use this feature only if you haven’t moved during this turn, and after you use it, your Speed is 0 until the end of the current turn.
- **Assassin Features**
	- **Initiative**. You have [Advantage](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#advantage_xphb) on [Initiative](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#initiative_xphb) rolls.
	- **Surprising Strikes**. During the first round of each combat, you have [Advantage](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#advantage_xphb) on attack rolls against any creature that hasn't taken a turn. If your Sneak Attack hits any target during that round, the target takes extra damage of the weapon's type equal to your Rogue level.
