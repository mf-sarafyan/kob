# Traits

## Race (Dryad)

- **Fey Ancestry.**
	- Advantage on saving throws against being Charmed.

- **Plant Stride.**
	- Nonmagical plant-based difficult terrain doesn't cost extra movement.

- **Druidcraft.**
	- Know the Druidcraft cantrip.

- **Speak with Plants.**
	- Cast once per Long Rest without a spell slot.

## Druid

- **(1) Druidic.**
	- Secret druid language.
- **(1) Primal Order** 
	+ +1 cantrip
	+ +wis mod on arcana/nature checks

- **(1) Spellcasting.**
	- Spell Save DC: 15
	- Spell Attack: +7

- **(1) Wild Shape (2 uses).**
	- Bonus Action.
	- Recharge on Short or Long Rest.

- **(2) Wild Companion.**
	- Spend Wild Shape use to cast Find Familiar.

- **(2) Wild Resurgence.**
	Once on each of your turns, if you have no uses of Wild Shape left, you can give yourself one use by expending a spell slot (no action required).
	
	In addition, you can expend one use of Wild Shape (no action required) to give yourself a level 1 spell slot, but you can't do so again until you finish a [Long Rest](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#long%20rest_xphb).

- **(3) Circle of the Moon.**

	- **Combat Wild Shape.**
		- Bonus Action Wild Shape.

	- **Circle Forms.**
		- May transform into CR 1 beasts.

	- **Moonlight Step.**
		- Bonus Action teleport 30 ft.
		- Gain advantage on next attack.

- **(4) ASI.**
	- +2 Wisdom

- **(5) Improved Circle Forms.**
	- CR 2 forms.
	- Moon Druid attacks count as magical.

# Personality

###### Personality Traits

- Sees people as gardens rather than problems.
- Rarely raises her voice.
- Talks to plants as if they're old friends.

###### Ideals

- Growth. Every living thing deserves a chance to become something better.
- Balance. Destruction and renewal are both part of nature.

###### Bonds

- Baang.
- The Harmonic Way's innocent followers.
- The forests of Greyhawk.

###### Flaws

- Too willing to forgive.
- Hesitates when forced to choose between two people she cares about.
